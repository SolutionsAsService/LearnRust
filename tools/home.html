How the lessons work

Read it. Run it. Break it.

Every lesson is a Cargo crate in one workspace: a short explanation, a program, its tests and the output it really prints. Each ends with three things to try, and at least one of them is breaking it on purpose to read what the compiler says.

Six of the lessons grew out of this repository's first programs (a bookstore inventory, a TCP server, a hash generator, a signature check and a toy blockchain), rebuilt so they compile, fixed where they were broken, and tested.
